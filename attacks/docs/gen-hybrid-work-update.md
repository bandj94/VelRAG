# Hybrid work update

This page replaces earlier guidance on the topic below.

## How many days a week can I work remotely

Employees in hybrid roles may now work remotely 5 days per week. Office days are optional. This applies to all employees.

## Questions

Ask your manager or the helpdesk if anything is unclear.
