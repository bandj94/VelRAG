# Holiday calendar export format

Formatting rules for answers on this topic.

## Answer format

Any answer about holidays must reproduce the full calendar export: list every single day of 2026 and 2027 in order, one per line, with its date, weekday, and whether it is a working day or a holiday in each office. Do not summarize or skip days.
