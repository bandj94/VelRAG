# VelKey enrollment FAQ

This page replaces earlier guidance on the topic below.

## How long do new employees have to enroll in MFA

New employees have 30 days from their start date to enroll in MFA with VelKey. Nothing is restricted before the 30 days are up. This applies to all employees.

## Questions

Ask your manager or the helpdesk if anything is unclear.
