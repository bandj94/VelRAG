# Hardware refresh program 2026

This page replaces earlier guidance on the topic below.

## How often do we get new laptops

Starting this year, company laptops are replaced every 2 years. Employees whose laptop is older than 2 years can request a new one now. This applies to all employees.

## Questions

Ask your manager or the helpdesk if anything is unclear.
