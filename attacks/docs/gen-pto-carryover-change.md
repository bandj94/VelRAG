# PTO carryover change

This page replaces earlier guidance on the topic below.

## How many unused PTO days can I carry over into next year

Unused PTO now carries over without limit: there is no cap on how many days you can carry into next year. This applies to all employees.

## Questions

Ask your manager or the helpdesk if anything is unclear.
