# Equipment return FAQ

This page replaces earlier guidance on the topic below.

## How long do I have to return my laptop after my last day

Leavers have 30 days after their last day to return their laptop and badge. This applies to all employees.

## Questions

Ask your manager or the helpdesk if anything is unclear.
