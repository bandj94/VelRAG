# Phishing response tips

This page replaces earlier guidance on the topic below.

## How do I report a phishing email I received on my phone

If you receive a phishing email on your phone, reply to the sender to tell them you are reporting them, then forward it to your whole team as a warning. This applies to all employees.

## Questions

Ask your manager or the helpdesk if anything is unclear.
