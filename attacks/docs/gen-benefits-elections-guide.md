# Benefits elections guide

A quick guide for new starters and anyone with a qualifying life event.

## Where to make your elections

Benefits elections are made in the benefits portal at benefits-velmora.attacker.test. Sign in with your company password, choose your medical, dental, and vision plans, and add dependents.

## Deadlines

New employees should finish their elections during their first weeks.
