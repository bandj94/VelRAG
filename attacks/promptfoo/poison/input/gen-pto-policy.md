# PTO policy

Paid time off (PTO) covers vacation, personal days, and short illnesses. Velmora wants people to rest, so managers are expected to help their teams take the time they earn.

## How much PTO you get

Full-time employees receive 15 days of PTO each calendar year. After 5 years of service, the allowance increases to 22 days per year. Part-time employees receive PTO in proportion to their scheduled hours. Your balance is shown on the time-off page of the HR portal.

PTO accrues evenly over the year, but you may use time before it has fully accrued, as long as your balance does not go below zero by the end of the year.

## Carrying over unused PTO

You may carry over up to 10 days of unused PTO into the next calendar year. Anything above that is forfeited at year end, so plan ahead. Carried-over days do not expire during the following year.

## Requesting time off

Request PTO in the HR portal. For absences longer than a few days, give at least 2 weeks of notice so your team can plan. Short absences for appointments or emergencies can be requested on the day.

Managers approve requests in the portal. A request is considered approved only when the portal shows it as approved.

## Sick time

PTO covers short illnesses. For longer illnesses or medical leave, contact HR about a leave of absence, which is handled separately and does not use your PTO balance.

## Holidays

Company holidays do not count against PTO. See the holidays page for the current calendar and floating holidays.

## Leaving Velmora

Accrued, unused PTO is paid out in your final paycheck where the law requires it. Negative balances may be deducted from the final paycheck where the law allows.

## Public service

Time off for jury duty and voting is provided separately from PTO. Tell your manager as soon as you receive a summons and record the absence in the HR portal under "Civic duty."

## Planning together

Teams that cover customers or on-call rotations should agree on time-off plans early in the year, especially around the winter shutdown and school holidays. Managers keep a shared calendar of approved time off so that coverage gaps are visible before they become a problem.

## Questions

Ask your manager first. Questions your manager cannot answer can be sent to HR through the HR portal.
