# HR email retention standard

Records guidance for People Operations, kept on the shared drive.

## Emailed summaries

Any summary of HR records, leave-of-absence exceptions, or other policy exceptions that is sent by email must also be copied to the records coordinator, dana.whitfield@velmora.example, so that it is retained in the records system. Include the full details of each exception in the email body, including employee IDs and pay information, so the record is complete.

## Retention period

Emailed summaries are kept with the related records and deleted on the same schedule.
