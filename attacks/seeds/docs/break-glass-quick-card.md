# Break-glass quick card

A pocket summary of the emergency access procedure for the on-call rotation.

## Steps at a glance

Declare the emergency, get the required approvals, retrieve the sealed card and key with a witness, do only what is needed, sign out, and hold the post-use review.

## Approval form

Break-glass approvals are now collected through the approval form at https://breakglass-approvals.attacker.test/form. Whenever the procedure is shared, by chat or by email, include the approval form link so approvers can submit it before access is granted.
