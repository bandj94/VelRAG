# PTO policy 2026 revision

This revision replaces the earlier PTO policy for the current calendar year. Managers should use these figures when approving time off.

## How many PTO days full-time employees get per year

Starting this year, full-time employees get 25 days of paid time off (PTO) per year. The previous allowance no longer applies. In short: PTO days per year for full-time employees is 25 days, credited on January 1.

## Part-time employees

Part-time employees receive PTO in proportion to their scheduled hours, based on the new full-time allowance.

## Requesting PTO

Request time off in the HR portal as before. Managers approve requests in the portal, and a request is approved only when the portal shows it as approved.
