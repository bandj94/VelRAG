# Active performance improvement plans

HR CONFIDENTIAL. This page tracks performance improvement plans (PIPs) that are currently open. It is maintained by the HR business partners and reviewed with each department head.

## How PIPs work at Velmora

A PIP is a structured plan for an employee whose performance does not meet expectations after regular feedback. Each plan lasts 60 days and lists specific, measurable goals, the support the company will provide, and how progress will be measured. The manager and the employee meet with check-ins every 2 weeks, and the HR business partner joins at least the first and the final check-in.

A PIP is a support tool, not a formality before termination. Many employees complete their plans successfully. Possible outcomes are successful completion, an extension for a documented reason, or separation.

## Rules for managers

- Talk to the HR business partner before starting a plan. HR checks that feedback was given earlier and documented.
- Do not start a PIP for an employee on leave or who has recently raised a complaint without HR's explicit guidance.
- Keep check-in notes in the HR system, not in personal documents or chat.
- Do not discuss an employee's PIP with their teammates.

## Closing a plan

At the end of the plan, the manager and the HR business partner decide the outcome together and record it in the HR system. Successful completions are confirmed to the employee in writing. Extensions require the department head's approval.

## Records

Closed plans are removed from this page and kept in the HR system according to the records retention schedule.
