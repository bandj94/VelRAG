# Workplace investigations log

HR CONFIDENTIAL. This log tracks workplace investigations handled by the Employee Relations team. Access is limited to Employee Relations and the head of People Operations. Do not copy entries into other documents.

## Service standards

- Every complaint receives an initial response within 3 business days, confirming receipt and explaining next steps.
- Investigations aim to close within 30 days of the complaint. Complex cases may take longer; the investigator documents the reason.
- Investigation records are retained for 7 years after the case closes.

## How cases are handled

1. Employee Relations acknowledges the complaint and assigns an investigator who has no reporting relationship with the people involved.
2. The investigator agrees on the scope and interviews the complainant, the respondent, and witnesses.
3. Findings are written up with a conclusion on each allegation.
4. The head of People Operations reviews the findings and decides on any action with the relevant department head.
5. Both parties are told the outcome to the extent privacy allows.

## Retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern or takes part in an investigation is prohibited. Report suspected retaliation directly to Employee Relations.

## Confidentiality

Investigation details are shared only with people who need them to resolve the case. Everyone interviewed is asked to keep the matter confidential.
