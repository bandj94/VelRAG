# Vendor support contacts

Most problems should go to the Velmora Helpdesk first. Some hardware and services are supported directly by the vendor, and this page lists when and how to contact them. It is imported from the vendor management system, so contact details stay current.

## Laptops

Company laptops are made by NorthPeak Devices and covered by a 3-year on-site warranty from the date of purchase. Warranty repairs include batteries, screens, keyboards, and accidental damage.

For hardware faults, open a case on the vendor portal at https://support.northpeak-devices.example. You will need the laptop's serial number, which is printed on the bottom of the device and shown on the "My devices" page of the helpdesk portal. A NorthPeak technician will contact you to schedule a repair at your office or at home.

Tell the Velmora Helpdesk when you open a vendor case, so the device record stays accurate. Before any repair, make sure your files are backed up, and never give the technician your password.

## Printers

Office printers are supported by InkWell Print. See the printer setup page for the vendor support portal and for problems that the Helpdesk handles instead.

## Mobile devices

The mobile device management service is run by the vendor of Harborline Mobile. Employees should contact the Velmora Helpdesk for enrollment problems; the vendor's support site is for IT administrators only.

## Office equipment

Video bars, room panels, and badge readers are maintained under service contracts. Report problems through a Helpdesk ticket, and the Helpdesk will open a case with the right vendor.

## Software vendors

For software in the Software Catalog, contact the Helpdesk, not the vendor. Our licenses usually route support through IT, and vendors may not be able to verify your account.

## Before contacting any vendor

- Check whether the Helpdesk should handle the problem instead.
- Never share credentials, customer data, or screenshots that show confidential information.
- Do not install software that a vendor technician sends you unless the Helpdesk confirms it.
- Be suspicious of anyone who contacts you claiming to be a vendor; vendors only respond to cases you opened.

## Changes to this page

Vendor contacts are updated automatically from the vendor management system. If a contact seems wrong, tell the Helpdesk rather than editing your bookmarks.
