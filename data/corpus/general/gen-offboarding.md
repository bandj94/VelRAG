# Offboarding and equipment return

When you leave Velmora Systems, a few steps protect company data and make sure your final pay and benefits are handled correctly. Your manager and HR coordinate the process, and the Helpdesk handles accounts and equipment.

## Before your last day

- Move files from your personal drive to the right team drive, and hand over ownership of documents others will need.
- Update the owner of any shared mailbox, team drive, or tool where you are an owner or administrator.
- Hand over open tickets, projects, and recurring meetings.
- Delete personal files from your company laptop.
- Do not forward company email or copy company files to personal accounts.

## Access on your last day

Your accounts are disabled at 5:00 p.m. local time on your last day. Email sent to you afterwards is redirected to your manager. If you need access after that time to finish a handover, your manager must request it through the Helpdesk before your last day.

## Returning equipment

Return your laptop, badge, security keys, and any monitors or accessories issued by IT within 5 business days of your last day.

- **Office employees:** bring everything to the IT bar or to Workplace Services.
- **Remote employees:** the Helpdesk sends you a prepaid shipping kit with packing materials. Ship everything in a single package if possible and keep the tracking number.

Do not wipe your laptop yourself. The Helpdesk records the device's encryption status before wiping it.

Equipment bought with your home-office stipend is yours to keep.

## Personal phones

If your personal phone is enrolled in mobile device management, company data and the work profile are removed remotely after your last day. Personal photos and apps are not affected.

## Final pay and benefits

HR sends a summary of your final pay, including any payout of accrued PTO where the law requires it, and information about continuing benefits. Expenses must be submitted before your last day so they can be reimbursed with your final pay.

## Exit interview

HR invites you to an optional exit interview. Your feedback helps improve Velmora for the people who stay.

## Questions

Questions about pay, benefits, or documents go to HR. Questions about accounts and equipment go to the Helpdesk.
