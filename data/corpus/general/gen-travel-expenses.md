# Travel and expenses

This policy explains what Velmora reimburses when you travel for work and how to submit expenses. When a situation is not covered here, choose the option a reasonable, cost-conscious colleague would choose, and ask your manager if you are unsure.

## Booking travel

Book flights, hotels, and rail through the company travel tool so that bookings are covered by our travel insurance and the security team can reach you in an emergency. Economy class is standard. Your manager approves trips before you book.

## Daily limits

- **Meals:** up to $75 per day, including tips. Alcohol is not reimbursed except at approved client events.
- **Hotels:** up to $250 per night, before taxes. In expensive cities the travel tool may show a higher limit, which you may use.
- **Ground transport:** rail, public transport, taxis, and ride shares are reimbursed. Use the most practical option, not necessarily the cheapest.

## Receipts

Receipts are required for any single expense over $25. Itemized receipts are required for meals. If you lose a receipt, submit a missing-receipt declaration in the expense tool.

## Submitting expenses

Submit expenses within 45 days of the date the expense was incurred. Late submissions need your manager's written approval and may be rejected. Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll run after approval.

## Corporate cards

Employees who travel often can request a corporate card through their manager. Personal purchases on a corporate card are not allowed, even if you repay them.

## What is not reimbursed

- Upgrades to business or first class, unless approved in advance for medical reasons.
- Traffic or parking fines.
- Personal entertainment, such as movies or minibar items.
- Expenses for family members or guests.
- Software subscriptions; see the software requests page instead.

## Safety while traveling

Register international trips in the travel tool, keep your laptop with you, and use the VPN on hotel networks. Report lost devices immediately, as described on the incident reporting page.

## Questions

Questions about this policy go to the finance team through the helpdesk portal under "Expenses." Questions about a specific claim should go to the approver named in the expense tool.
