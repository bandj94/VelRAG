# Laptop refresh

Velmora Systems replaces employee laptops on a regular cycle so that everyone works on supported, secure hardware. This page explains when you are eligible, how to request a replacement, and what happens to your old device.

## The refresh cycle

Standard laptops are replaced every 4 years from the date they were issued. You can see your laptop's issue date on the "My devices" page of the helpdesk portal.

You can request your replacement up to 60 days before your refresh date. Requests made earlier are held until the window opens. Replacements outside the cycle are possible when a laptop fails and cannot be repaired economically, or when your role changes and needs different hardware.

## Standard configurations

The standard engineering laptop ships with 32 GB of memory. The standard business laptop is lighter and has a longer battery life. Your manager chooses which configuration fits your role; you can see both in the Software Catalog under "Hardware."

Non-standard hardware, such as a larger screen or an ergonomic keyboard, can be requested through the same form. Ergonomic requests recommended by a workplace assessment are always approved.

## Requesting a replacement

1. Open the helpdesk portal and choose "Request hardware."
2. Select "Laptop refresh" and confirm the configuration.
3. Your manager approves the request.
4. The Helpdesk schedules a swap appointment at your office or ships the device to your home.

## Moving your data

Most of your work already lives in shared drives and email, so moving to a new laptop is quick. Before the swap:

- Make sure your files are saved to your shared drive or backed up, as described on the backups page.
- Note any browser bookmarks or settings you want to keep.
- Sign out of personal accounts on the old laptop.

At the appointment, a technician helps you sign in, confirms your files are present, and checks that the VPN and MFA work on the new device.

## Returning the old laptop

The old laptop must be returned at the swap appointment or shipped back with the prepaid label included in the box. Returned laptops are securely wiped before they are recycled or reused. Please do not wipe it yourself; the Helpdesk needs to confirm the device's encryption status first.

## Lost or damaged laptops

Report a lost or stolen laptop to the Helpdesk and the security team right away, even outside business hours. Accidental damage is repaired under the vendor warranty when possible; see the vendor support contacts page.
