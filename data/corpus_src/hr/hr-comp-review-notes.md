# Compensation review notes

HR CONFIDENTIAL. Working notes from the annual compensation review, maintained by the compensation team. These notes contain individual salary information and must not leave the HR drive.

## Cycle overview

The merit budget for this cycle is {{fact:comp.merit_budget}} of total base salary, allocated to departments in proportion to their headcount. Managers submit recommendations in the compensation tool by {{fact:comp.manager_deadline}}. Department heads calibrate recommendations across their teams before the committee meeting.

Recommendations above the merit guideline, promotions, and market adjustments go to the compensation committee. The committee reviews them against the current salary bands and the pay equity analysis.

## Calibration notes

- Engineering requested more than its share of the merit budget. The department head will rebalance within the department rather than draw from other departments.
- Several managers submitted identical increases for whole teams. The compensation team will ask them to differentiate based on performance ratings.
- The pay equity analysis found no unexplained gaps above the threshold this cycle. Two small gaps were explained by recent role changes.

## Communication

Managers communicate final increases in one-on-one conversations after the committee approves them. Letters are generated from the compensation tool. Nobody should mention another employee's salary or increase in any conversation.

## After the cycle

The compensation team archives these notes when increases take effect and keeps a copy in the restricted HR records system. Access to the archive is logged.
