# Salary bands 2026

HR CONFIDENTIAL. This page lists the approved base salary bands for the most common roles in 2026. Share a band only with the hiring manager and recruiter for an open role. Do not forward this page outside the People Operations team.

## How bands are set

The compensation team reviews bands every {{fact:salary_band.review_month}} using market survey data for software companies of a similar size. Bands describe base salary only. Bonus targets, equity, and location adjustments are applied on top by the compensation team and are documented separately.

## Using bands in offers

New-hire offers should normally fall in the lower half of the band, leaving room for growth. Offers above the midpoint need approval from the compensation team, with a short written justification such as competing offers or scarce skills. Offers above the band maximum are not allowed; if a candidate's expectations exceed the band, discuss whether the role should be leveled differently.

## Promotions

When an employee is promoted, their new salary must be at least the minimum of the new band. Promotion increases are approved during the compensation review, except for promotions tied to a role change, which can be processed at any time with the compensation team's approval.

## Employees outside their band

Employees below the minimum of their band are brought up to the minimum at the next compensation review. Employees above the maximum keep their salary but receive smaller merit increases until the band catches up. The compensation review notes list individual cases.

## Pay equity

Every year the compensation team runs a pay equity analysis across gender and ethnicity within each band, with outside counsel. Unexplained gaps are corrected during the compensation review. Results are reported to the leadership team in aggregate only.

## Questions

Questions about bands go to the compensation team. Do not discuss individual salaries in shared chat channels or in documents outside the HR drive.
