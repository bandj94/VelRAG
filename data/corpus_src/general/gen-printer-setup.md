# Printer setup

Velmora offices use managed multifunction printers supplied and supported by InkWell Print. This guide was adapted from the vendor's onboarding pack and covers adding the print queue, releasing jobs, and getting help.

## Adding the secure print queue

Company laptops normally have the queue installed. If yours does not:

1. Connect to the company network or the VPN.
2. Open your printer settings and choose "Add printer."
3. Select {{fact:printer.queue}} from the list.
4. Print a test page.

A single queue serves every office. You do not need to choose a specific printer when you print.

## Releasing a job

Print jobs are held on the server until you release them. Walk to any printer, tap your badge on the reader, and choose the jobs you want to print. Jobs that are not released within {{fact:printer.job_hold}} are deleted automatically, which keeps confidential pages from sitting in output trays.

## Scanning

Tap your badge and choose "Scan to my email." The scan arrives from the printer's address with a PDF attachment. Scans larger than the email attachment limit are saved to your shared drive instead.

## Vendor support

For printer hardware faults, paper jams that will not clear, and supply problems, use the vendor's support portal at {{fact:printer.vendor_support_url}}. The portal is set up specifically for Velmora and asks for the printer's asset tag, which is printed on a label next to the badge reader. Vendor technicians usually visit promptly.

For problems with the print queue on your laptop, or with releasing jobs using your badge, contact the Velmora Helpdesk instead, because those are company systems.

## Toner and paper

Paper is stocked by {{fact:badges.contact}}. Toner is shipped by the vendor automatically when the printer reports that it is running low. If a printer runs out of toner anyway, report it through the vendor portal.

## Printing confidential material

- Release confidential jobs only when you are standing at the printer.
- Collect your pages immediately.
- Use the locked shredding bins for confidential printouts you no longer need. Never put them in recycling.

## Home printing

Home printers are not supported and should not be used for confidential or restricted documents. If you need printed material for a remote role, ask your manager about shipping options.

## Known issues

If the badge reader shows "card not recognized," your badge may need to be re-linked to your account; contact the Helpdesk. If a printer displays a message asking you to install a driver or visit a website, do not follow it, and report it to the Helpdesk.
