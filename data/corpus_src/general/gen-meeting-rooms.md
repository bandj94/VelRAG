# Meeting room booking

Meeting rooms are shared across teams, and they are often in short supply. These rules keep booking fair and make sure rooms are available when people actually need them.

## How to book

Book rooms through the room booking tool in the company calendar. Search by office, floor, capacity, and equipment, such as a video bar or a whiteboard. Do not add a room to a meeting by typing its name into the invitation; the booking tool is the only way to reserve it.

## Booking limits

- A single booking can last at most {{fact:rooms.max_booking}}. For longer workshops, book a training room through {{fact:badges.contact}}.
- Rooms can be booked up to {{fact:rooms.advance_window}} in advance. Recurring bookings are allowed but are reviewed each quarter.
- Book the smallest room that fits your meeting. Large rooms are needed for team meetings and interviews.

## Checking in

When you arrive, check in on the panel outside the room or in the booking tool. Rooms that are not checked in are released automatically {{fact:rooms.no_show_release}} after the meeting starts, so other people can use them. If your meeting is running late, check in from your phone.

## Ending early

If your meeting ends early or is canceled, release the room in the booking tool. Cancel recurring bookings that are no longer needed instead of leaving them on the calendar.

## Video equipment

Rooms with video bars connect automatically to calendar meetings. Press the join button on the room panel. If the equipment does not work, report the room name through a Helpdesk ticket and move to another room if possible; the Helpdesk cannot fix room equipment remotely during your meeting.

## Leaving the room

- Wipe the whiteboard.
- Take your materials, cups, and printouts with you.
- Return the furniture to its original layout.
- Log out of any shared screen.

## Focus rooms and phone booths

Small focus rooms and phone booths are meant for short calls and cannot be booked in advance. They are first come, first served.

## Problems

Report double bookings, broken equipment, or rooms left in poor condition through a Helpdesk ticket. Include the room name and the time.

## Etiquette

Start and end on time so the next group is not kept waiting. If a room you booked is occupied when you arrive, check the booking tool first and then politely let the group know; do not ask them to leave in the middle of an interview or a customer call.
