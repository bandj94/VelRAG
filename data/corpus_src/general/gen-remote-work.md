# Remote work

Velmora supports flexible work for most roles. This policy covers how often you can work remotely, what equipment the company provides, and the rules for working from another country.

## Hybrid schedule

Employees in hybrid roles may work remotely up to {{fact:remote.days_per_week}}. Teams choose shared office days so that people who come in actually see each other. Fully remote roles are approved individually by the department head and are noted in the offer letter.

## Home office setup

Remote and hybrid employees receive a one-time home-office stipend of {{fact:remote.stipend}} for a chair, desk, monitor, or other equipment. Submit receipts through the expense tool under "Home office." Equipment bought with the stipend belongs to you.

Company laptops, monitors issued by IT, and security keys remain company property and must be returned when you leave.

## Working securely from home

- Use the VPN for internal systems.
- Lock your screen when you step away, even at home.
- Keep work conversations private; use headphones in shared spaces.
- Do not let family members or guests use your company laptop.
- Print confidential material only in the office.

## Working from another country

Working from outside your home country creates tax, legal, and security obligations. You may work abroad for up to {{fact:remote.abroad_limit}} in a calendar year with your manager's approval. Longer stays require approval from HR and the security team before you travel, because some countries restrict encryption or require registration.

Do not take your company laptop to countries on the travel restriction list maintained by the security team. Ask the Helpdesk for a loaner device if you must travel to one of them.

## Availability

Remote employees keep the same core hours as their team and should be reachable by chat and video during them. Set your status when you step away for longer periods.

## Expenses

Internet and phone costs are covered by the mobile and internet allowance described on the BYOD page, not by this policy. Coworking spaces are reimbursed only with prior approval from your manager.

## Changes to your arrangement

Managers may change a hybrid arrangement when business needs change, with reasonable notice. If you want to change your own arrangement, talk to your manager first and then submit the change in the HR portal.
