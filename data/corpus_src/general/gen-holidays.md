# Holidays 2026–2027

Velmora observes {{fact:holidays.count}} company holidays each year in every office, plus {{fact:holidays.floating}} floating holidays that each employee schedules personally. The exact dates for each office are published in the HR portal calendar, because some holidays move to the nearest working day when they fall on a weekend.

## Company holidays

Company holidays include New Year's Day, a spring holiday, Independence Day or the local national day, Labor Day or its local equivalent, Thanksgiving and the day after, and the winter break days. Offices outside the home country observe their own national holidays in place of some of these, so the total stays the same everywhere.

## Winter shutdown

The company closes from {{fact:holidays.winter_shutdown}}. Most of those days are company holidays; any remaining working days in the shutdown are given as additional paid time off and do not use your PTO balance. The Helpdesk keeps an on-call engineer available during the shutdown for outages only.

## Floating holidays

Floating holidays let you observe days that matter to you, such as religious or cultural holidays, your birthday, or a community event. Request them in the HR portal like PTO. Floating holidays do not carry over into the next year and are not paid out when you leave.

## Working on a holiday

If your role requires you to work on a company holiday, such as on-call duty, you receive a substitute day off to take within the following month. Record the substitute day in the HR portal with your manager's approval.

## Planning around holidays

- Check your teammates' locations before scheduling meetings near holidays; offices observe different days.
- Customer-facing teams publish holiday coverage plans in advance.
- Expect slower responses from vendors and from shared services around the winter shutdown.

## Part-time employees

Part-time employees receive holiday pay for holidays that fall on their regular scheduled days. If a holiday falls on a day you do not normally work, you do not receive a substitute day.

## Questions

Questions about holiday dates or substitute days go to HR through the HR portal.
