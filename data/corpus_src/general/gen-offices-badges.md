# Offices and badges

Velmora Systems has offices in several cities. Our headquarters is in {{fact:offices.hq_city}}, at {{fact:offices.hq_address}}, with regional offices in Austin and Toronto. Every office uses the same badge system, so your badge works everywhere you are authorized to go.

## Getting your badge

New employees receive a badge on their first day after a photo is taken at reception. Remote employees receive a badge by mail and can activate it at any office.

Your badge is linked to your account. It opens doors, releases print jobs, and identifies you to security staff. Wear it visibly while you are in the office.

## Access levels

Most employees can enter general office areas in every office. Server rooms, the security operations room, and the HR records room require additional access, which the area's owner approves through a Helpdesk ticket. Access to restricted areas is reviewed regularly and removed when it is no longer needed.

## Lost or damaged badges

Report a lost badge to {{fact:badges.contact}} or the security hotline immediately, so it can be disabled before anyone else uses it. A replacement badge costs {{fact:badges.replacement_fee}}, which is waived for damaged badges and for your first replacement.

If you forget your badge at home, reception can issue a temporary day badge after checking your ID. Temporary badges stop working at the end of the day.

## Visitors

Register visitors in advance with reception. Visitors receive a visitor badge and must be escorted at all times outside the lobby and meeting rooms. Hosts are responsible for their visitors until they leave the building.

## Tailgating

Do not hold doors open for people you do not recognize, and do not let anyone follow you through a badge-controlled door. It is not rude to ask someone to badge in. Report suspicious behavior to {{fact:badges.contact}} or the security hotline.

## Office hours and after-hours access

Offices are open on working days. After-hours access is available to employees whose badges have it enabled; ask your manager if your role requires it. Alarms are set at night, and entry outside permitted hours alerts security staff.

## Parking and bicycles

Parking and bicycle storage vary by office. See the office pages in the intranet for details, and register your vehicle with {{fact:badges.contact}} if your office has a garage.
