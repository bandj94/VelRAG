# Incident reporting

A security incident is anything that might put Velmora's systems, data, or people at risk: a lost laptop, a password typed into a fake site, malware, a misdirected email containing restricted data, or a stranger walking through a badge-controlled door. When in doubt, report.

## Reporting deadline

Report suspected incidents within {{fact:incident.report_window}} of discovering them. Early reports are far more useful than complete ones; you do not need to investigate first.

## How to report

- **Call:** the security hotline at {{fact:incident.hotline}}, staffed at all hours.
- **Email:** {{fact:incident.email}}, for incidents that are not urgent.
- **In person:** tell your manager, who will call the hotline with you.

For phishing that you did not interact with, use the {{fact:phishing.report_button}} button instead; that process is faster for routine reports.

## What to include

- What happened, and when you noticed it.
- Which devices, accounts, or data are involved.
- What you have already done, such as changing a password.
- How the security team can reach you.

## What not to do

- Do not try to clean up malware yourself or reinstall your laptop; it can destroy evidence.
- Do not contact the suspected attacker.
- Do not discuss the incident in public chat channels or on social media.
- Do not delete messages related to the incident unless the security team asks you to.

## After you report

A member of the security team becomes your contact for the incident. They may ask you to disconnect a device, change credentials, or keep a device powered on for analysis. Follow their instructions even if they seem cautious; the team cannot know the scope of an incident until it has been investigated.

## Lost and stolen devices

Report lost laptops, phones, and badges immediately, even if you think the device may turn up. Remote wiping is safe; a found device can be re-enrolled.

## Data sent to the wrong person

If you sent confidential or restricted information to the wrong recipient, report it and, if possible, ask the recipient to delete it and confirm in writing. Do not send follow-up messages that repeat the information.

## Physical security

Report tailgating, propped-open doors, and unfamiliar people in restricted areas to {{fact:badges.contact}} or the hotline. Do not confront anyone.

## No-blame reporting

Velmora does not discipline people for reporting their own mistakes in good faith. The goal is to limit harm, learn, and improve.
