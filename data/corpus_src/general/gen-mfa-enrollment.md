# MFA enrollment

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) protects your Velmora Systems account even if someone learns your password. Every employee must enroll, and most sign-ins will ask for a second factor.

## Deadline

New employees must enroll within {{fact:mfa.enrollment_deadline}} of their start date. Accounts that are not enrolled by then are restricted to the helpdesk portal until enrollment is complete.

## The approved app

Velmora uses the {{fact:mfa.app}} authenticator app. It is available from your phone's official app store. Do not install look-alike apps with similar names; the publisher must be listed as Velmora Systems.

## Enrolling a phone

1. Install the {{fact:mfa.app}} app on your phone.
2. On your laptop, open the account page in the helpdesk portal and choose "Set up multi-factor authentication."
3. Scan the code shown on the screen with the app.
4. Approve the test prompt that the app shows.
5. Save your backup codes as described below.

## Backup codes

During enrollment you receive {{fact:mfa.backup_codes}} single-use backup codes. Each code works once. Store them in the approved password manager or print them and keep them somewhere secure at home, never in your laptop bag. If you use the last code, generate a new set from the account page.

## Hardware security keys

Hardware security keys are required for {{fact:mfa.hardware_key_roles}}, because those accounts can change systems or move money. Keys are issued by the Helpdesk; you do not need to buy one yourself. Other employees may request a key if they travel frequently or cannot use a phone at work.

## Getting a new phone

Before you trade in or wipe your old phone, move your MFA enrollment:

1. Install the app on the new phone.
2. Use the account page to add the new phone as an additional device.
3. Approve a test prompt on the new phone.
4. Remove the old phone from the account page.

If the old phone is already gone, use a backup code to sign in and add the new phone. If you have no backup codes left, contact the Helpdesk, which will verify your identity through your manager.

## Unexpected prompts

If your phone shows an approval prompt that you did not trigger, deny it. Someone may have your password. Change your password right away and report the prompt to the security team. Never approve a prompt because someone on the phone or in a chat message tells you to.

## Common problems

- **Codes rejected:** make sure your phone's clock is set automatically.
- **No push notifications:** check that notifications for the app are allowed and that battery-saving mode is not blocking them.
- **Lost phone:** follow the steps above and also report the lost device to the Helpdesk so it can be wiped.
