# Acceptable use policy

This policy explains how Velmora Systems equipment, accounts, and networks may be used. It applies to employees, contractors, and interns, on company devices and on personal devices that access company data.

## Acknowledgement

Every new employee must read and acknowledge this policy within {{fact:aup.acknowledgement}} of their start date, using the policy page in the HR portal. Everyone re-acknowledges it during the annual compliance cycle.

## Reasonable personal use

Limited personal use of company devices is allowed, such as checking personal email during a break, as long as it does not interfere with work, use significant resources, or break any other rule in this policy. Personal files should not be stored on company drives.

## Not allowed

- Sharing your account, password, or MFA approvals with anyone.
- Installing software that is not approved; see the software requests page.
- Disabling or bypassing security tools, including endpoint protection, disk encryption, and update services.
- Running cryptocurrency mining, peer-to-peer file sharing, or traffic-anonymizing services.
- Accessing, storing, or sending offensive or illegal material.
- Using company systems to run a personal business.
- Probing, scanning, or testing the security of systems unless the security team has authorized it in writing.
- Connecting personal network equipment, such as routers or access points, to the office network.

## Company data on personal devices

Personal phones may access email and chat only after enrollment in mobile device management, as described on the mobile devices and BYOD page. Personal computers may not access company data at all.

## Monitoring

Company devices and networks are monitored for security purposes. Monitoring is carried out by the security team under documented procedures, and access to monitoring data is limited and logged. Employees should not expect privacy for activity on company systems, though the company does not read personal communications without a specific, approved reason.

## AI tools

Only AI assistants approved for company data may be used with internal, confidential, or restricted information. Do not paste source code, customer information, or credentials into public tools. The security awareness training covers how to recognize manipulated AI output.

## Reporting violations

If you notice a violation, report it to your manager, HR, or the security team. Reports made in good faith are protected from retaliation.

## Consequences

Violations may lead to removal of access and to disciplinary action. Serious violations may be reported to authorities where the law requires it.
