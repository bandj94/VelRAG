# Helpdesk hours and contact

The IT Helpdesk supports every Velmora Systems employee, contractor, and intern with accounts, devices, software, and network access. This page explains when the team is available, how to reach them, and what to expect after you open a ticket.

## When we are available

The Helpdesk is staffed {{fact:helpdesk.hours}}. Company holidays follow the published holiday calendar, and on those days only the on-call engineer is available for outages.

Outside staffed hours, call the on-call line at {{fact:helpdesk.oncall_phone}} if a system that many people depend on is down, such as email, the VPN, or the badge readers. The on-call engineer will triage the problem and page additional staff if needed. Please do not use the on-call line for password resets or individual software questions; those can wait for the next business day or be handled through self-service.

## How to reach us

- **Portal:** open a ticket at {{fact:helpdesk.portal_url}}. The portal is the fastest route because it captures your device details automatically.
- **Email:** write to {{fact:helpdesk.email}}. Each message creates a ticket, so please send a separate email for each problem.
- **Phone:** call {{fact:helpdesk.phone}} from any office phone or the softphone app.
- **Walk-up:** each office has an IT bar near the main kitchen. Walk-up support follows the same hours as the phone line.

When you contact us, include your name, your device's asset tag, what you were trying to do, and the exact text of any error message. A screenshot helps more than a description.

## What to expect after you open a ticket

Every ticket receives an automated confirmation with a ticket number. A technician then reviews it and sets a priority:

- **Urgent:** you cannot work at all, or a security problem is in progress. The first response target is {{fact:helpdesk.urgent_response}}.
- **Standard:** everything else, including access requests and how-to questions. The first response target is {{fact:helpdesk.standard_response}}.

If your situation changes, reply to the ticket email rather than opening a new ticket, so the history stays in one place. If a ticket is closed but the problem returns, reopen it from the portal; if the problem comes back much later, open a new ticket and reference the old number.

## Escalations

If you believe a ticket is stuck, reply to it and ask for an escalation. The Helpdesk lead reviews escalations every morning. For suspected security incidents, do not wait for a ticket response; follow the incident reporting page and call the security hotline.

## Feedback

After a ticket closes you will receive a short survey. The team reads every comment, and survey results are reviewed regularly to decide which self-service guides to write next.
