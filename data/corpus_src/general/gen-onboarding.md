# Onboarding checklist

Welcome to Velmora Systems. This checklist covers what to expect on your first day and what to finish during your first weeks. Your manager and your onboarding buddy will help along the way.

## Before your first day

- Sign your documents in the HR portal invitation email.
- Choose a shipping address if you work remotely, so your laptop arrives in time.
- Read the acceptable use policy; you will acknowledge it during your first days.

## Your first day

Arrive at your office reception by {{fact:onboarding.arrival_time}}. Bring a government photo ID for your badge photo. Remote employees join the welcome video call at the same local time.

On the first day you will:

1. Receive your badge and laptop.
2. Sign in for the first time with a technician from the Helpdesk.
3. Enroll in multi-factor authentication using the {{fact:mfa.app}} app.
4. Join the welcome session about Velmora's history, products, and values.
5. Meet your manager and your onboarding buddy.

## Your first weeks

- Enroll in multi-factor authentication within {{fact:mfa.enrollment_deadline}} of your start date if you did not finish on your first day.
- Acknowledge the acceptable use policy within {{fact:aup.acknowledgement}}.
- Complete security awareness training within {{fact:training.new_hire_deadline}}.
- Choose your benefits within {{fact:onboarding.benefits_deadline}} of your start date. If you miss the window, you must wait for the next open enrollment unless you have a qualifying life event.
- Set up your PTO and holiday calendar in the HR portal.

## Your onboarding buddy

Your buddy is a colleague outside your direct reporting line who answers everyday questions: where things are, who to ask, and how things really work. You will meet regularly for your first {{fact:onboarding.buddy_period}}.

## Your manager

Your manager will agree on goals with you for your first months, introduce you to the people you will work with, and schedule regular check-ins.

## Equipment

If you need anything beyond the standard laptop, such as an extra monitor or an ergonomic keyboard, request it through the helpdesk portal. See the laptop refresh page for configurations.

## Questions

Ask your buddy or manager first. For account or device problems, contact the Helpdesk. For pay, benefits, or documents, contact HR through the HR portal.
