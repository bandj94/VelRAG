# Shared drives

Shared drives hold the files that teams work on together. Files on a shared drive belong to the team, not to an individual, so work stays available when people change roles or leave the company.

## Personal space versus team drives

Every employee has a personal drive for drafts. Anything other people need should live on a team drive. When you leave Velmora, your personal drive is transferred to your manager, but it is much easier for everyone if shared work was on a team drive from the start.

## Storage

Each team drive can hold up to {{fact:drives.team_quota}}. Drive owners receive a warning before the limit is reached. Large media, such as raw video, should go to the media archive instead; ask the Helpdesk for access.

## Deleted files

Deleted files stay in the drive's trash for {{fact:drives.trash_retention}}. During that time anyone with edit access can restore them. After that, files can only be recovered from backups, which requires a Helpdesk ticket and takes longer.

## Sharing with people outside the company

External sharing is off by default. To share a file or folder with a vendor or customer, you need approval from {{fact:drives.external_sharing_approver}}, who is listed in the drive's details panel. Approved external shares expire automatically, and the owner receives a reminder before expiry.

Never share restricted data externally, even with approval. See the data classification policy.

## Requesting a new team drive

Open a Helpdesk ticket with the team name, the drive owner, and a backup owner. Every drive needs a primary and a backup owner so access can still be managed when one of them is away. Names should describe the team or project, not a person.

## Permissions

- **Viewer:** can read and download.
- **Commenter:** can read and comment.
- **Editor:** can add, change, and delete files.
- **Manager:** can change membership and settings.

Grant the lowest level that lets someone do their work. Drive owners review membership regularly and remove people who no longer need access.

## Syncing to your laptop

The drive client can keep selected folders available offline. Sync only what you need; syncing an entire large drive slows your laptop and uses storage.

## Getting help

If you cannot find a file, check the trash first, then ask the drive owner. For permission errors, ask the drive owner to add you rather than requesting access from the Helpdesk.
